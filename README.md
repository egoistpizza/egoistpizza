### Yusuf Özçetin

I research parameter-efficient deep learning, currently compressing segmentation networks with quantum-inspired tensor networks (matrix product states). Double-major undergraduate in Electrical & Electronics Engineering and Computer Engineering at the Turkish-German University, Istanbul.

**Open to** research assistant and lab internship positions in efficient ML, tensor networks or computer vision.

#### Research

- **[hybrid-qtn](https://github.com/egoistpizza/hybrid-qtn)**: replaces the 1024-channel bottleneck of a U-Net with a tensor-train-factorized Axial MPS block, cutting bottleneck parameters by 91.8 % and the whole network's by 44.9 %. Evaluated on polyp segmentation (Kvasir-SEG, CVC-ClinicDB), road extraction and camouflaged object detection, three seeds per configuration, reproducible via Docker. Team project; I am the lead researcher and developer.
- **[movement-segmentation-poc](https://github.com/egoistpizza/movement-segmentation-poc)**: proof of concept for adding instance segmentation masks to [`movement`](https://github.com/neuroinformatics-unit/movement), an animal-behaviour analysis library. Detects social interactions from SAM 2 masks with morphological dilation. Written for my Google Summer of Code 2026 proposal.

#### Other work

- **[stv-election-cli](https://github.com/egoistpizza/stv-election-cli)**: exact-arithmetic Single Transferable Vote counter implementing the ERS Weighted Inclusive Gregory Method. Used for the TDU InformatiX General Assembly election.
